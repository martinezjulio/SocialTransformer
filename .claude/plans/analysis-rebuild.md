# Plan: analysis rebuild v2 (pipeline)

Written 24 Oct 2026 from `.claude/plans/meta-plan.md`, which holds every decision and its reasoning;
section numbers in brackets (e.g. [MP 2.4]) point there. This file replaces the first
`analysis-rebuild.md`. Companion: `preregistration.md` (the document the pipeline's numbers pin).

## Context

The analysis lives in a 2,700-line notebook (`notebooks/analysis.py`) on a thin module
(`src/analysis/policy.py`, `descriptive.py`), with a numpyro descriptive model, WAIC comparison, no
recovery, no free-running PPC, and a round parameter. Advisor feedback moved the confirmatory
primaries to a descriptive model and the policy models to secondary. The notebook is kept runnable as
a separate, legacy thing; a new pipeline is built beside it on pilot E and will run unchanged on the
full experiment. Its outputs end in a generated results file for the paper.

## Decisions carried from the meta-plan (summary)

- Legacy: `notebooks/analysis.py` stays; its module moves to `notebooks/legacy_analysis/`; outputs
  under `$OUTPUT_DATA_DIR/legacy_analysis/`; no cross-imports with `src/analysis/` [MP 1].
- Primary: H1, H2, H3, H5 on payoff and entropy per play game (reward reported, no rule; the former H4 exploratory, 28 Oct), `lmer` + `emmeans` in R on
  `outcome ~ condition * play * round + (1 | participant)`, condition ∈ {expert-first, novice-first,
  solo}, solo play = slots 2 and 4; two-sided α = 0.05 with sign, Holm within hypothesis [MP 2].
- Secondary, story order: S1 whose belief (S1a recent, S1b novice), S2 a readout, not counts (S2a last-visible, S2b expert-visible, S2c naive), S3 social beats own (S3a H+Social > H+Own, S3b H+Both > H+Own), every part ΔELPD > 2 SE and all parts required; S4 Δ logit ω_N (95% ETI excludes 0). Primary H4 (was H6 until 28 Oct): the partner choice (glmer). Story order: full model → whose belief →
  belief not copying → ablations → switch → unseen expert → partner choice [MP 3].
- Two rounds asserted; synthetic test-set evaluation stays in `eval.py` [MP 4]. Pilot E is the
  development set; descriptive stage reads `preprocessed_participant_data.csv` (inference skips solo) [MP 5].
- Exclusions in `preprocess_participant_data.R` on anonymised data; `anonymize_participant_data.py` uncommitted [MP 2.7, 5.5].
- Unseen-expert section: readout table, copying-belief comparison, ELPD split (supplementary),
  exploit statistic; eight-weight switch model for Fig 4(a) [MP 6, 8.4].
- 95% intervals everywhere (recovery: 95% intervals cover the truth in ≥ 22 of 25 datasets; PPC: 95% band); PSIS-LOO; free-running PPC; recovery only for the S1a, S2a and S4 models [MP 3.4, 10.1].
- Results: `results.tex` macros + `results.md` + tables + figures, manifest-checked [MP 9].

Outputs: `$OUTPUT_DATA_DIR/analysis/<version>/{games,descriptive,choice,checks,unseen,figures,paper}/`.
Inputs: `$EXPERIMENT_DATA_DIR/<version>/preprocessed_participant_data.csv` (all sessions) and
`$OUTPUT_DATA_DIR/eval/<version>/data/*.csv` (social sessions; read-only, from `run_inference.sh`).

Invariants: simulated sessions use the human trial schema (recovery and PPC reuse the fitting code);
no module under `src/` imports `notebooks/`, none under `notebooks/` imports `src/analysis`; the
pipeline asserts 2 rounds, 4 social / 8 solo play games per session, 6 shots per play game.
No eager imports: every `__init__.py` under `src/analysis/` and `src/inference/` is a docstring only
(import submodules directly); each module imports only what it uses, at the top, and nothing that
pulls in wandb, the training data loaders or `train.py` (shared pieces such as the segment mask live
in small modules, e.g. `models/masking.py`); JAX and numpyro are imported only by the stage 5–6 modules
that need them, so the descriptive and games stages load without them.

## Runtime (from the 5.6 benchmark, 25 Oct; GPU)

Tables win. Per likelihood gradient (stand-in H+Both, lapse-mixed): trilinear table lookup 0.6 ms at pilot size,
9.8 ms at ~266 participants, i.e. ~1 min / ~20 min per fit of 4 chains × 1000 draws × ~30 leapfrogs. Table-free
quadrature at the accuracy the likelihood needs (Simpson CDF, 2049 nodes on [-40, 40]: |Δp| 6e-5) costs 75 ms /
527 ms per gradient, 100× more, and JAX's betainc is 35 rows/s with no gradient in (a, b). Table build with the
scipy betainc quadrature (legacy-exact, 1e-6; threaded, ~2.7k rows/s): ~7 min at pilot size, ~49 min at 266
participants, once per dataset and grid, cached (0.11 / 0.76 GB). Core run on pilot E: ~16 fits × ~1 min plus the
build; full experiment: ~16 × 20 min plus the build, under a day in all; checks (recovery + PPC) are the
larger cost and stay cached. Real NUTS ESS/s is confirmed at 5.16 (parity fits).

Revised 26 Oct after 5.10–5.15 (cubic gathers, dense grid, measured fits). Per full-grid table: pilot ~45 min, 300 participants ~10 h, 500 ~17 h on 16 CPU threads; five sources for the choice stage. Fits: H+Both 4 min at pilot size (4 vectorised chains, rung 0), ~30 min at 300; 21 models. Choice stage first run: ~5 h pilot, ~1.5 days at 300 on this box; reruns ~45 min / ~7 h. Stage 6 is the real cost: every simulated dataset needs its own tables (two sources for Switch-by-round), ~20 h each at 300 with the scipy builder, so recovery (25 datasets) is infeasible here without the GPU builder (5.20, ~10×) and smaller recovery datasets (open decision 5). On Sherlock (`owners`: ~1000 GPUs, 2-day jobs, preemptible; `hns` 7-day for the assembly) with 9.9: units of ≤ 2 h as requeueable array tasks; the 300-participant pipeline is about a day of wall time including recovery.
